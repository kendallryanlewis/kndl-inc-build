1. Executive Summary

Pixel & Post is a brand + web studio helping local businesses launch or level‑up their online presence with clean design, fast builds, and measurable growth. We sell one‑time build packages plus monthly management to drive recurring revenue. Our differentiator: enterprise‑grade engineering polish (Angular/.NET expertise) with small‑studio speed and service.

2025 Objectives
  • Close 6–10 new build projects across Starter/Growth/Pro tiers.
  • Grow to 12+ monthly retainers at $400–$2,500+/mo.
  • Ship reusable templates/components to reduce build hours by 25–40%.
  • Showcase 3 flagship case studies (before/after SEO, lead gen, revenue lift).

2. Company Overview

  • Name: Pixel & Post (KNDL Inc.)
  • What we do: Brand identity, websites (WordPress/Wix/Angular), on‑page SEO, content & social setup, print collateral, analytics, and paid acquisition frameworks.
  • Edge: Proven enterprise delivery (Orica, AA Union platform) translated into SMB‑friendly packages; full‑stack capability when custom development is needed.
  • Positioning: Total brand presence, measurable results.

3. Market & Customer Segments

Primary: Local SMBs (home services, clinics, gyms, eateries, professional services), solo founders, and community orgs that need credibility + leads fast.
Secondary: Mid‑size firms needing custom apps/portals (Angular + .NET) and ongoing support.

Buyer pains: Outdated sites, weak branding, low search visibility, no content strategy, no time to manage marketing.

Personas:
  • The Owner‑Operator: Wants calls booked; hates tech. Values simplicity, clear ROI.
  • The Office Manager: Needs repeatable workflows (updates, posts, reviews) and reporting.
  • The Growing Team: Needs scalable site, SEO content plan, and lightweight CRM/automation.

4. Value Proposition

  • Launch fast: Clean, SEO‑friendly builds with clear CTAs and simple editing.
  • Look credible: Cohesive brand kit + on‑brand collateral (web + print).
  • Be found: On‑page SEO + Google Business Profile setup; starter content plan.
  • Grow smart: Optional monthly management, analytics, and ad frameworks.
  • Flex up: When needed, we deliver custom Angular/.NET apps with databases and integrations.

5. Service Catalogue & Tiers

Starter — “Brand Essentials” ($325–$780)

For businesses just getting started with a brand presence.

Includes
  • Logo refresh
  • 1–2 social profiles
  • One‑page SEO‑friendly website
  • Business card + brief brand guide
  • Google Business optimization
  • SEO homepage copy
  • 1 month starter social posts
Optional Monthly Management ($195–$390/mo)
  • Google Business updates (posts, reviews)
  • 4–6 social posts/month
  • Basic analytics report
Build target: 5–10 hours. Price aligns to $50–$60/hr equivalent.

Growth — “Digital + Print” ($1,040–$1,950)

For small businesses ready to market online and offline.

Includes
  • Full brand kit (logos, colors, fonts)
  • 3–4 social profiles with banners & highlights
  • Multi‑page website
  • Print materials (cards, flyers, postcards)
  • 1 month of content templates
  • On‑page SEO optimization
  • 1 blog or 1 video
Optional Monthly Management ($520–$910/mo)
  • 8–12 social posts/month
  • Monthly blog/article or video for SEO
  • 2 ad campaigns (Google/Meta)
  • Email list setup + campaign
  • Growth + analytics report
Build target: 16–30 hours. Price aligns to $50–$60/hr equivalent.

Pro — “Total Brand Presence” ($2,600–$4,550+)

For teams needing a custom site and a cohesive brand push.

Includes
  • Custom website (WordPress/Wix/Angular)
  • Full brand kit + collateral
  • 3 months social content (setup & templates)
  • Advanced print (banners, signage, promo items)
  • Advanced SEO strategy & ranking setup
  • Email + SMS automation setup
  • Paid ad campaign framework
Optional Monthly Management ($1,300–$3,250+/mo)
  • Full social media management (posting + engagement)
  • Weekly blog or video content
  • Paid ad campaigns (Google, Meta, retargeting)
  • Email + SMS campaigns
  • Influencer/partnership outreach
  • Advanced SEO + ranking reports
  • ROI dashboard + monthly strategy call
Build target: 40–70+ hours depending on custom dev; price scales with scope.

6. Add‑Ons & Upsells (Any Tier)

Digital Growth
  • Reputation management (reviews): $100–$300/mo
  • Local SEO domination: $500–$1,200 setup + $300–$600/mo
  • Marketing automation (CRM, email/SMS workflows): $500+ setup
  • Retargeting ads (Google + Meta pixels): $200–$500/mo mgmt
Creative & Brand
  • Photography packages: $400+/session
  • Video ads/reels: $250–$800 each
  • Branded merch (shirts, mugs, pens, stickers): cost + markup
  • Landing pages & funnels: $250–$600/page
Strategy & Consulting
  • Analytics dashboards (Looker Studio): $200–$500 setup + $100–$300/mo
  • Influencer/partnership outreach: $300–$700/mo

7. Pricing Strategy (One‑time + Recurring)

  • Anchor: Internal rate of $50–$60/hr. Package price ranges reflect estimated hours + margin for management, QA, and revisions.
  • One‑time builds: Collect 50% deposit; 40% at design approval; 10% at launch.
  • Recurring: Month‑to‑month (cancel anytime) or 6‑month commitment (‑10% price).
  • Scope guardrails: One round of revisions included per milestone; change requests billed hourly or via mini‑add‑ons.
  • Maintenance: Offer WordPress care plans (backups, updates, security) at $75–$200/mo depending on site size and plugins.
Non‑Development Service Rates (hourly)
  • Strategy & consulting: $75–$100/hr
  • Design/UI & prototyping: $55–$70/hr
  • Content/copywriting/editing: $45–$65/hr
  • SEO & analytics/reporting: $55–$85/hr
  • Project management & meetings: $35–$50/hr
  • QA/testing & cross‑browser checks: $45–$60/hr
  • Admin/data entry/asset sourcing: $25–$35/hr
  • Rush/emergency (nights/weekends): 1.5×–2× applicable hourly rate
Approx. Hour → Price Mapping (internal)
  • 6 hrs → ~$360
  • 12 hrs → ~$720
  • 24 hrs → ~$1,440
  • 48 hrs → ~$2,880
  • 60 hrs → ~$3,600

8. Delivery & Operations

Tech Stack
  • Web: WordPress/Wix; Angular + .NET for custom builds; Azure App Service + SQL; CI/CD via Azure DevOps.
  • Design: Figma; Adobe CC; shadcn/ui + Tailwind for React/Next prototypes when needed.
  • SEO/Content: Google Search Console, Analytics 4, Looker Studio dashboards.
Standard Workflow
  1. Discover (1–2 hrs): Goals, audience, refs, sitemap, KPIs.
  2. Brand (4–12 hrs): Logo/refresh, palette, type, patterns, brand guide.
  3. Site (6–40+ hrs): Wireframes → design → build → QA → launch.
  4. SEO & Content (2–10 hrs): On‑page, metadata, schema, 1–2 pieces of content.
  5. Launch & Care Plan enrollment.
SLA
  • Support response within 1 business day. Critical issues: same day.
  • Turnaround: Starter (≤1 week), Growth (1–3 weeks), Pro (3–6 weeks+ custom dev).
Templates/Reuse
  • Reusable WordPress starter theme, Elementor/Block templates, and content packs.
  • Angular component library for common marketing sections; deploy via private npm.

9. Sales & Marketing Plan

Acquisition Channels
  • Google Business Profile + local SEO; citations.
  • Portfolio site with case studies and lead magnets (checklists, pricing guide PDF).
  • Social proof: before/after visuals, client testimonials, GMB reviews.
  • Partnerships with print shops, photographers, and co‑working hubs.
  • Outreach: local chambers, Facebook groups, LinkedIn, and cold/warm email.
Funnel
  • Top: Free “Local Visibility Audit” (automated report) → email capture.
  • Middle: 15‑min discovery call → proposal within 48 hours.
  • Bottom: Deposit to reserve start date → onboarding form + asset upload.
Collateral
  • One‑page “Client Version” of tiers (see Section 15).
  • Slide deck with 3 case studies and ROI snapshots.

10. Financial Model (Assumptions & Projections)

Assumptions
  • Effective rate: $55/hr mid‑range.
  • Capacity: ~60 production hours/month solo (alongside other commitments).
  • Close rate: 25–35% on qualified leads.
Targets
  • Builds/month: 1 Starter + 1 Growth (+ Pro every 1–2 months).
  • MRR goal: $4k–$8k from 10–15 retainers.
Example Month (@55/hr)
  • 1 Starter (8 hrs → $440) + 1 Growth (24 hrs → $1,320) + 0.5 Pro (30 hrs → $1,650)
  • One‑time revenue: ~$3,410
  • Recurring (10 retainers avg $600): $6,000
  • Total monthly: ~$9,410 (before costs)
Costs (ballpark)
  • Hosting/SaaS/plugins: $150–$300/mo
  • Subcontractors (as needed): $50–$70/hr pass‑through + margin
  • Ads/marketing: $100–$300/mo

11. Implementation Roadmap (Next 90 Days)

Weeks 1–2
  • Finalize website + portfolio; publish 2 case studies (Orica/AA Union adapted for SMB).
  • Create tier one‑pagers, proposal template, and onboarding checklist.
Weeks 3–6
  • Launch lead magnet + email nurture (3‑email sequence).
  • Outreach to 30 local businesses; book 6 discovery calls.
  • Build WordPress starter theme + page templates.
Weeks 7–12
  • Close 3–4 build projects; enroll at least 6 retainers.
  • Ship 1 Looker Studio KPI dashboard template.
  • Gather testimonials and publish before/after metrics.

12. KPIs & Reporting

  • Acquisition: Discovery calls booked, proposals sent, close rate.
  • Delivery: Cycle time per tier, revision count, on‑time launches.
  • SEO: Impressions, CTR, top‑10 keywords, local pack visibility.
  • Content: Posts/month, blog/video output, engagement rate.
  • Revenue: One‑time vs MRR mix, ARPU, churn, gross margin.

13. Risks & Mitigations

  • Scope creep: Strict revision limits; change orders.
  • Capacity crunch: Reusable templates; vetted subcontractors on call.
  • Plugin/security issues: Care plans; staging + backups; update windows.
  • Lead drought: Maintain weekly outreach cadence; partner referrals.

14. Legal, Tax & Admin

  • Entity & taxes: LLC (KNDL Inc.), EIN, business bank, bookkeeping.
  • Contracts: MSA + SOW per tier; IP ownership on full payment; portfolio rights.
  • Payments: Invoices with staged milestones; retainer autopay.
